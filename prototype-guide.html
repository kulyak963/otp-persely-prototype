Persely prototype guide

Everything that is in the Team 3 prototype for OTP Bank: what each screen does, the rules behind the numbers, how to run the demo, and where to change things in the code. Read this to review the prototype and to propose what to add before Friday.

OTP × Corvinus seminar · Team 3Demo data onlyEnglish UI

The idea

Problem from OTP: students leave when they turn 24 and the OTP Junior package ends. Revolut is close behind OTP among young people (consideration 64% vs 61%).

Our answer: a new Persely tab inside the real OTP app. Persely already exists (340,000+ users, 0.01% interest). We give students a reason to save with OTP (3.0% student rate), a habit (weekly streak), a face (Persi, the green Persely piggy from OTP's own ads) and a reason to stay after 24 (the graduation bridge).

From the OTP research
  • 85% say saving early matters, only 27% use a savings product
  • Young people are cautious: show "withdraw any time, no fees, insured" everywhere
  • Income is irregular until about 25: weekly streak, any amount counts
  • Short, clear texts; no forced jokes
From Levente's handoff
  • 3.0% student rate, 28% tax, first 500,000 Ft
  • Payday rule that asks first, round-ups, weekly transfers
  • Shared Persely, euro Persely, graduation bridge
  • Monthly insight on Home, semester recap, goal forecast
From the team
  • 1:1 copy of the OTP app, the new tab in their style
  • Persi in the tab and in notifications
  • Snapchat-style streak with a flame
  • Colourful monthly stories, "PS5 + GTA 6" goal

Open the prototype

Online, for everyone

otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com — the same app, hosted on Render, updated automatically from the GitHub repository kulyak963/otp-persely-prototype. On an iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The free server sleeps after 15 idle minutes, so open the link a minute before presenting. Presenter tools online need the presenter key: …/?key=ADMIN_KEY once on the laptop, and …/push.html?key=ADMIN_KEY for the push panel (ask Denis for the key).

On the laptop

  1. Start the server: node server.js in the project folder (port 8090).
  2. Open http://localhost:8090. On a wide screen the app sits in an iPhone frame, and the demo panel is on the left.
  3. Presenter panel for real pushes: http://localhost:8090/push.html (only works on this laptop).

On an iPhone

  1. Run the tunnel: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090. It prints an https address like …trycloudflare.com.
  2. Open that address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from the icon → More → Notifications → Turn on notifications.

The free tunnel gets a new address every time it restarts. Then the app has to be added to the Home Screen again.

One Persely screen

Tap any goal.

  • Balance, progress, goal date
  • "At your pace you get there in April 2027" or "after your goal date"
  • Add money / Take out
  • Rate on this goal in Ft per year
  • Switches: payday rule, round-ups, weekly transfer go to this goal
  • Shared: who put in what, rules, Invite a friend
  • Euro: "buy euros bit by bit"
  • Last 5 activity lines

Create a goal (3 steps)

New → Your own goal, or a preset.

  1. Name, amount, date
  2. Weekly transfer and round-ups
  3. Check: "With these rules: about 111,000 Ft by 1 September", short by X, Make it 3,000 Ft a week, Create

Sheets

  • Add money: goal chips, amount, from Junior or Savings account, streak hint
  • Withdraw: instant, no fee, streak not affected; shared = only your part
  • Payday: "+75,000 Ft arrived. Move 10% (7,500 Ft)?" with before → after
  • Payday rule / Weekly / Round-ups settings
  • Verify: 0.01% vs 3.0% in Ft, Verify with Neptun

Graduation screen

"Congratulations, Anna" (or "Your Junior package ends soon"). Three steps: give employer your OTP account number, first salary arrives at OTP within 6 months, Perselys keep 3.0% for 12 months. Tip to raise the payday rule. Keep my 3.0% turns the badge into Graduate 3.0%.

Persi and notifications

Persi is our own 3D version of the green Persely piggy (three.js). It appears in the tab header, the update pop-up, the onboarding summary (a coin drops in), the graduation screen, the story and the Home card. It speaks in one short, friendly line, without slang. Persely notifications are signed "Persi · Persely". On the laptop the banner shows Persi's portrait; a real iPhone always shows the app icon.

How it is built

No framework, no database. The whole app is one HTML file that runs in the browser; a tiny Node server serves it and sends push notifications. State lives in the browser (localStorage key otp-clone-en-v5), so every phone has its own demo.

FileWhat it is
index.htmlThe whole app: styles, screens, rules, onboarding, story, 3D piggy, demo panel.
server.jsNode server on port 8090. Serves only listed files. Push API: /api/vapid, /api/subscribe, /api/test; laptop-only /api/push, /api/subs, /api/clear.
sw.jsService worker: offline cache and showing push notifications. Bump the cache name after changes.
push.htmlPresenter panel for real pushes to all connected phones (laptop only).
manifest.webmanifest, icons, splashInstall on the Home Screen with the OTP icon and loading screen.
three.min.js3D library for Persi.
data/Push keys and subscribed phones. Never served.
demo-script.html, prototype-guide.htmlThe demo script and this guide.

After any change: reload the laptop page; on the iPhone swipe the app away and open it again. Changing the localStorage key resets everyone's demo.

Limits to know

  • Demo only: no real accounts, money, Neptun check or bank backend.
  • The free tunnel can stop and changes its address; the laptop alone always works.
  • iPhone notifications need the app on the Home Screen and notifications turned on inside the app.
  • Interest is credited monthly in the demo; in the proposal it accrues daily.
  • Weekly challenges exist in the code but are hidden from the tab to keep it short.
  • Persi is our own model; for the real app OTP's official artwork should replace it.
  • English UI; the real app is Hungarian.

How to propose a change

Write which screen, what should change and why (ideally with the research reason). Small text or number changes take minutes; new screens take longer.
