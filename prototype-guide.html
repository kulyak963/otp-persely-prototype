Persely prototype guide

Everything that is in the Team 3 prototype for OTP Bank: what each screen does, the rules behind the numbers, how to run the demo, and where to change things in the code. Read this to review the prototype and to propose what to add before Friday.

OTP × Corvinus seminar · Team 3Demo data onlyEnglish UI

The idea

Problem from OTP: students leave when they turn 24 and the OTP Junior package ends. Revolut is close behind OTP among young people (consideration 64% vs 61%).

Our answer: a new Persely tab inside the real OTP app. Persely already exists (340,000+ users, 0.01% interest). We give students a reason to save with OTP (3.0% for every Junior customer, automatically), a habit (weekly streak), a face (Persi, the green Persely piggy from OTP's own ads) and a reason to stay after 24 (the graduation bridge).

From the OTP research
  • 85% say saving early matters, only 27% use a savings product
  • Young people are cautious: show "withdraw any time, no fees, insured" everywhere
  • Income is irregular until about 25: weekly streak, any amount counts
  • Short, clear texts; no forced jokes
From Levente's handoff
  • 3.0% rate, 28% tax, first 500,000 Ft (we give it to every Junior customer, no sign-up)
  • Payday rule that asks first, round-ups, weekly transfers
  • Shared Persely, euro Persely, graduation bridge
  • Monthly insight on Home, semester recap, goal forecast
From the team
  • 1:1 copy of the OTP app, the new tab in their style
  • Persi in the tab and in notifications
  • Snapchat-style streak with a flame
  • Colourful monthly stories, "PS5 + GTA VI" goal

Open the prototype

Online, for everyone

otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com — the same app, hosted on Render, updated automatically from the GitHub repository kulyak963/otp-persely-prototype. On an iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The free server sleeps after 15 idle minutes, so open the link a minute before presenting. Presenter tools online need the presenter key: …/?key=ADMIN_KEY once on the laptop, and …/push.html?key=ADMIN_KEY for the push panel (ask Denis for the key).

On the laptop

  1. Start the server: node server.js in the project folder (port 8090).
  2. Open http://localhost:8090. On a real desktop screen (wide, tall, with a mouse) the app sits in an iPhone frame, and the demo panel is on the left. On any phone, also in landscape, there is no frame; a phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi.
  3. Presenter panel for real pushes: http://localhost:8090/push.html (only works on this laptop).

On an iPhone

  1. Run the tunnel: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090. It prints an https address like …trycloudflare.com.
  2. Open that address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from the icon → More → Notifications → Turn on notifications.

The free tunnel gets a new address every time it restarts. Then the app has to be added to the Home Screen again.

Sheets

Graduation screen

"Congratulations, Anna" (or "Your Junior package ends soon"). Three steps: give employer your OTP account number, first salary arrives at OTP within 6 months, Perselys keep 3.0% for 12 months. Tip to raise the payday rule. Keep my 3.0% turns the badge into Graduate 3.0%.

Analyst and health score

It answers the analyst promise in the opening script: Persi stops being only a tip card and helps the student plan the month.

FeatureWhat it does
Analyst, offered in contextOff by default (opt-in). It is offered where it helps: on a late goal's page, on Persi's monthly tip on Home, under My monthly spending on Home ("See how it fits your income"), on the Monthly spending page, and with a switch in More (the switch turns it off in one tap). Every entry opens one sheet. Its consent bullets: what it measures (card payments, transfers, direct debits and income on this OTP account; your regular bills and a guess for next month; your monthly split: Essentials, Fun and Saving, and one insight a month), and that the names of people you pay are only used to sort your payments. It then shows the suggested Essentials 50% / Fun 40% / Saving 10% (37,500 / 30,000 / 7,500 Ft) from the last 3 months of card payments and income. Turn on and use this split (2 taps in all), or "Only turn it on, I will choose later", or change the percentages. Undo for 5 seconds.
My splitA card in the Persely tab, shown only when the analyst is on. Three buckets with live use, e.g. "Fun: 70% used, 12 days left". An edit sheet starts from the suggestion: Essentials 50% (37,500 Ft), Fun 40% (30,000 Ft), Saving 10% (7,500 Ft) of a 75,000 Ft salary. The Saving bucket is the payday rule, so the two always show the same number.
RecategoriseWith the analyst on, card payments in Transactions are tappable. The student can move one to another category, and the split and Persi's tips follow.
Financial health score0–100, shown as the bottom row of the Your habits card ("N of 100 · Financial health score · ▲ +N since last month"); tap it for the four indicators and the Rewards list. Four indicators, 25 points each, over a rolling 3 months: spends less than earns; no overdraft or failed payment; a buffer of about one month of spending; saves regularly toward a named goal.
RewardsEarned on improvement or on staying within the split, never on balance size. For students: boosted partner cashback (5% next month, up to 2,000 Ft) and goal-linked partner offers (reaching Summer holiday: travel partner, 8% back on one booking, up to 10,000 Ft; any other goal: 5% boosted cashback, up to 2,000 Ft) (a fee waiver would only make sense for OTP GO customers, 24–28, because Junior accounts are already free).
Score-change storyPart of the "Your progress" slide in the monthly story (analyst on and split accepted): the health score counts up from last month's to this month's, next to the goal ring and the streak.

Proposal only: the split percentages, the score design, the rewards and all rates are team proposals, not OTP facts. Demo order: turn on the analyst after the first deposit, then press O a month later. With the split accepted, Anna lands Essentials 36,900 of 37,500 Ft and Fun 27,000 of 30,000 Ft. Messages come one at a time: Persi's push "Reward earned…" (it also goes to the iPhone if Also send to iPhone is on), then the "Reward earned" sheet (score 50 → 75, 5% partner cashback next month), then the summary toast. The monthly story shows the score on its progress slide.

Bills, next month and Safe to spend

Financial analytics on top of the analyst (plan section 14). Everything here needs the analyst on, only tells and never blocks: no card is declined, nothing is held and no money moves without a tap. Only this OTP account is seen, so cash and money moved to other apps show as unknown.

Screen or sheetWhat it does and where it opens
Next month (page)Opens from the "Bills and next month" block in the Persely tab (under My split) and from the link row under My monthly spending on Home. A guess for the coming month from the last 3 finished months (recent months count more): expected income (regular salary and family transfer plus the lowest of the other incoming transfers), a bar and list that add up to 100% (Must-pay bills, Everyday essentials, Fun, Other, Saving, Left over), the categories with a small 3-month trend, and one-off payments that are left out (for example MediaMarkt, more than 3 times the usual). With fewer months it says "Rough estimate, gets better each month". Demo figures: about 138,000 Ft in, must-pay 61,000 Ft (44%), everyday essentials 36,900 Ft (27%), Fun 27,000 Ft (20%), Other 3,500 Ft, Saving 7,500 Ft, left over 2,100 Ft. These come from the demo history and are not real data.
Must-pay bills (page)Same block, and a link on Home. Regular payments found in the last 3 months: the same payee, one payment each month, 25 to 35 days apart, amounts within 15%. The demo has 6 must-pay bills (rent share 42,000 Ft, electricity 3,600, gas 2,400, internet 4,000, BKK student pass 3,450, phone 5,550 = 61,000 Ft); Netflix and Spotify are found too but count as Fun. The student confirms the list once and can switch each bill on or off. Each row has a status: Paid, Paid less than usual, Due in N days, Due today, Not paid yet.
Bill (sheet)Tap a bill: usual amount, due day, months seen, last payment, the must-pay switch, and Pay now, Already paid elsewhere, Not due this month.
Pay now (sheet)A prefilled transfer (payee, reference, amount from the last payment). It pays from the Bills pocket while that holds enough, otherwise from the Junior account. Nothing is sent until Pay 42,000 Ft is tapped. Undo for 5 seconds.
Safe to spend (sheet)Opens from the row under the balance on Home and from "Safe to spend now" in My split. Formula: Junior account balance − bills still to pay (less what the Bills pocket covers) − the saving rule not yet moved this month − groceries kept free (the lowest of the last 3 months). The Bills pocket balance is held apart and never counted. A guide, not a limit. Demo start: 87,450 − 5,550 − 3,500 − 18,560 = 59,840 Ft.
Payment (sheet)With the analyst on, tap a payment in Transactions: date, type, category, a "Repeats monthly" badge, the must-pay switch and Change category.
Bills first (card and alert)An orange card on Home and on the Persely tab when a must-pay bill is not paid after its date window, or one is due within 7 days and the balance does not cover it: "Bills first: 47,550 Ft still to pay", with Pay now, All bills, Already paid elsewhere, Not due this month. The Fun bar in My split says "Bills first: … to pay". Persi says the same line, and one push a day at most after a Fun card payment. When the bills are paid Persi says "Bills paid. Fun is back on."
Payday: Bills first (sheet)When a bill is open, the payday sheet (key P) shows Salary +75,000 Ft, Bills still to pay −47,550 Ft, Saving rule −7,500 Ft and Left for Fun 19,950 Ft, with Move 61,000 Ft to Bills pocket and the usual Move 7,500 Ft to the goal.
Bills pocketA pot for bills only on the Must-pay bills page: no goal, no interest, held outside the Junior balance. Moving money in is an own-account move (it is not spending and not income). "Move 61,000 Ft to Bills pocket" is a month of must-pay bills; it can be moved back at any time.

Nothing is counted twice: every transaction is either income, spending in one category, an own-account move (Persely, Savings, Bills pocket, interest), cash or a top-up to another app. Own-account moves are never income and never spending. The self-check window.__checkAnalytics() in the browser console runs the checks: all must pass.

Demo data and proposals: the 3-month history behind these screens (August to October, about 60 payments a month, and the bills) is made-up demo data, the same on every Reset. The projection weights, the 7-day and 15% limits, the groceries rule and the Bills pocket are team proposals, not OTP facts and not bank data.

Monthly story

Eight slides, one idea each, on one deep-green palette; every figure is computed from the transactions and the analytics, nothing is typed in. 1 the month with Persi · 2 in vs out (two bars, the gap is what is left over) · 3 where it went (donut: must-pay, essentials, Fun, Saving) · 4 bills (a receipt: "All 6 bills paid" or "Bills first: X Ft still to pay" with a Pay now button, plus the Bills pocket balance when it is on) · 5 the biggest change, only when a category is 25% or more above its 3-month average, otherwise the story has seven slides · 6 what you put aside (a stack of notes and coins, with rules, round-ups and interest) · 7 your progress (goal ring, streak, health score, and a goal that is behind with its fix) · 8 next month (the projected bar and one tip with a Set it up button that sets the monthly rule), then a share card without amounts. Slide length is 2.5 s plus 0.2 s per word on screen (at most 5 s), so the whole story runs about 30 to 35 seconds (the title says the computed figure). Tap right or left to move, press and hold to pause, swipe down to close; there is also a pause button. Slides 4 and 8 need the analyst (it is opt-in): with it off they are left out, and slide 8 keeps only the tip. The friends rank is no longer in the story. After "End of semester" it becomes the semester story: the same slides over the semester, with slide 5 showing the best and the hardest month.

How it is built

No framework, no database. The whole app is one HTML file that runs in the browser; a tiny Node server serves it and sends push notifications. State lives in the browser (localStorage key otp-clone-en-v5), so every phone has its own demo.

FileWhat it is
index.htmlThe whole app: styles, screens, rules, onboarding, story, 3D piggy, demo panel.
server.jsNode server on port 8090. Serves only listed files. Push API: /api/vapid, /api/subscribe, /api/test; laptop-only /api/push, /api/subs, /api/clear.
sw.jsService worker: offline cache and showing push notifications. Bump the cache name after changes.
push.htmlPresenter panel for real pushes to all connected phones (laptop only).
manifest.webmanifest, icons, splashInstall on the Home Screen with the OTP icon and loading screen.
three.min.js3D library for Persi.
data/Push keys and subscribed phones. Never served.
demo-script.html, prototype-guide.htmlThe demo script and this guide.

Limits to know

  • Demo only: no real accounts, money or bank backend.
  • Phone layout for every touch screen; the iPhone frame only on a real desktop (min 760 × 700 px with a mouse). The app is portrait only: sideways it shows "Turn your phone upright". Pinch zoom and larger text sizes work, and forms lift above the keypad (keyboard-safe sticky buttons, inputs at 16 px).
  • The free tunnel can stop and changes its address; the laptop alone always works.
  • iPhone notifications need the app on the Home Screen and notifications turned on inside the app.
  • Interest is credited monthly in the demo; in the proposal it accrues daily.
  • Weekly challenges exist in the code but are hidden from the tab to keep it short.
  • Undo restores the whole state saved before the action, so anything else changed in those 5 seconds is undone too; a push already sent cannot be taken back.
  • Persi is our own model; for the real app OTP's official artwork should replace it.
  • English UI; the real app is Hungarian.

How to propose a change

Write which screen, what should change and why (ideally with the research reason). Small text or number changes take minutes; new screens take longer.
