Persely prototype guide

Everything that is in the Team 3 prototype for OTP Bank: what each screen does, the rules behind the numbers, how to run the demo, and where to change things in the code. Read this to review the prototype and to propose what to add before Friday.

OTP × Corvinus seminar · Team 3Demo data onlyEnglish UISimple Persely on by default

The idea

Problem from OTP: students leave when they turn 24 and the OTP Junior package ends.

Our answer: a new Persely tab inside the real OTP app. Persely already exists (340,000+ users, 0.01% interest). We give students a reason to save with OTP (3.0% for every Junior customer, automatically), a habit (weekly streak), a face (Persi, the green Persely piggy from OTP's own ads) and a reason to stay after 24 (the graduation bridge).

From the OTP research
  • 85% say saving early matters, only 27% use a savings product
  • Young people are cautious: show "withdraw any time, no fees, insured" everywhere
  • Income is irregular until about 25: weekly streak, any amount counts
  • Short, clear texts; no forced jokes
From Levente's handoff
  • 3.0% rate, 28% tax, first 500,000 Ft (we give it to every Junior customer, no sign-up)
  • Payday rule that asks first, round-ups, weekly transfers
  • Shared Persely, euro Persely, graduation bridge
  • Monthly insight on Home, semester recap, goal forecast
From the team
  • 1:1 copy of the OTP app, the new tab in their style
  • Persi in the tab and in notifications
  • Snapchat-style streak with a flame
  • Colourful monthly stories, "PS5 + GTA VI" goal

Open the prototype

Online, for everyone

otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com — the same app, hosted on Render, updated automatically from the GitHub repository kulyak963/otp-persely-prototype. On an iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The free server sleeps after 15 idle minutes, so open the link a minute before presenting. Presenter tools online need the presenter key: …/?key=ADMIN_KEY once on the laptop, and …/push.html?key=ADMIN_KEY for the push panel (ask Denis for the key).

On the laptop

  1. Start the server: node server.js in the project folder (port 8090).
  2. Open http://localhost:8090. On a real desktop screen (wide, tall, with a mouse) the app sits in an iPhone frame, and the demo panel is on the left only with the demo flag: open http://localhost:8090/?demo=1 (remembered in the browser; ?demo=0 turns it off), or tap "Persely prototype" at the bottom of Settings five times. On any phone, also in landscape, there is no frame; a phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi.
  3. Presenter panel for real pushes: http://localhost:8090/push.html (only works on this laptop).

On an iPhone

  1. Run the tunnel: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090. It prints an https address like …trycloudflare.com.
  2. Open that address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from the icon → More → Notifications → Turn on notifications.

The free tunnel gets a new address every time it restarts. Then the app has to be added to the Home Screen again.

Goal page

Tap a jar twice, or a goal card on More.

  • On top: the Goal reached card when reached; saved of target and the bar; one date sentence ("At this pace you get there in November 2027. That is after your date."); the late or behind fix buttons; Add money and Withdraw; the honesty line
  • Settings for this goal (one tap): the rate line ("This goal earns about 1 380 Ft a year, after tax."; an empty goal says "Add money and it starts to earn 3.0%."), the goal date and the weekly figure, the goal's own saving rules (Payday rule %, Weekly transfer amount and day, Round-ups, each with Undo), Edit and Delete (Delete with money asks where it goes, with Undo)

Rate and safety (one sheet)

Replaces Good to know, Why Persely? and the rate part of How Persely works; every old entry opens it. "You get 3.0% a year on up to 500 000 Ft. It ends on [date]." 28% tax, about 2.2% kept, paid every month; withdraw any time, for free; keep 3.0% for 12 more months if the first salary comes to OTP within 6 months, otherwise the standard rate after 30 days' notice; others get 0.01%; deposit protection in one line (CONFIRM WITH OTP: scheme and limit). The per 100 000 Ft bars after tax: Persely 3.0% 2 160 Ft, Savings account 3.5% 2 520 Ft, old 0.01% 7 Ft, and "The Savings account pays a bit more: 3.5%. Persely pays 3.0%. Both are taxed. Persely keeps each goal apart. You can withdraw any time, for free." Needs OTP legal review before real use.

Saving rules, per goal

Unchanged by Simple Persely; notes in docs/per-goal-rules-notes.md.

  • Each goal has its own Payday rule (5 to 20%), Weekly transfer (1 000 to 5 000 Ft, a day) and Round-ups, edited under Settings for this goal with a 10 second Undo
  • Payday %s of all goals add up to at most 50% of the salary; weekly transfers add up; a round up coin is split equally; the 20 000 Ft floor applies to the total
  • Defaults for new goals: Settings > Persely > Default saving rules for new goals (the first goal: Payday rule 10%, asks first)

Sheets

  • Add money: last amount chosen, "From Junior account · Change", goal chips with 2 or more goals, the button names the amount ("Add 2 000 Ft")
  • Withdraw: empty amount with chips and "Withdraw all" (asks first); instant, no fee, streak not affected
  • Money move safety: New transfer is checked under each field; a money out above 20 000 Ft shows a review line with Confirm and Back; one Undo chip for 10 seconds that reverses only that move
  • Payday: "Salary · today, +75 000 Ft arrived", with open bills "Cover bills and save 7 500 Ft", "Only cover the bills", "Not this time"

One message at a time: banner, then sheet, then toast.

Your 3.0% rate (Keep my 3.0% request)

"Your 3.0% ends on [date]" with its conditions. Request to keep 3.0% opens a confirm sheet; Confirm makes it pending ("Waiting for your first salary at OTP") until the salary arrives (key P, or the next simulated month); then Graduate 3.0% and a push. Bills, next month and Safe to spend

Financial analytics on top of the analyst (plan section 14). Bills and Safe to spend work with the analyst on or off (off: the simpler estimate); the plan and categories need it on. Everything here only tells and never blocks: no card is declined, nothing is held and no money moves without a tap. Only this OTP account is seen, so cash and money moved to other apps show as unknown.

Bills pocketA pot for bills only on the Must-pay bills page: no goal, no interest, held outside the Junior balance. Moving money in is an own-account move (it is not spending and not income). The payday sheet moves only what is unpaid.

Nothing is counted twice: every transaction is either income, spending in one category, an own-account move (Persely, Savings, Bills pocket, interest), cash or a top-up to another app. Own-account moves are never income and never spending. The self-check window.__checkAnalytics() in the browser console runs the checks: all must pass (277 of 277 with the flags on, 242 with ?otphome=0, 212 with ?simple=0, on 20 November 2026).

Demo data and proposals: the 3-month history behind these screens (August to October, about 60 payments a month, and the bills) is made-up demo data, the same on every Reset. The projection weights, the 7-day and 15% limits, the groceries rule and the Bills pocket are team proposals, not OTP facts and not bank data.

Persi and notifications

Persi is our own 3D version of the green Persely piggy (three.js). In the simple app it is one voice at a time: one sentence on the Persely tab (priority list above), the Home card, the reward, the story and the graduation screen. No hint strips. Persely notifications are signed "Persi · Persely"; Perks offers are signed "Perks" and are not in Persi's channel. On the laptop the banner shows Persi's portrait; a real iPhone always shows the app icon.

One Persely push a day (Phase 3a, ?simple=0 turns it off): all Persely push types count together per day of the app date. A second one is written to the inbox only, with no banner, sound or real push; in demo mode a toast says "Held by the 1 a day limit". Reset day (panel, Z) lets one land again. Not counted: card payments and incoming transfers (the bank's own pushes) and the late bill push (its own limit). Quiet hours put a push silently in the inbox without using the day. Priority when several are due at once: 1 payday, 2 Junior ends, 3 goal milestone, 4 Sunday reminder (opt in), 5 story, 6 the rest (interest, rewards), 7 marketing. Every type has its own switch; Offers and perks is off by default. There is no friend push, no day 0 push and no "we miss you" push, and no urgency words.

How it is built

No framework, no database. The whole app is one HTML file that runs in the browser; a tiny Node server serves it and sends push notifications. State lives in the browser (localStorage key otp-clone-en-v5), so every phone has its own demo.

Limits to know

  • Demo only: no real accounts, money or bank backend.
  • Phone layout for every touch screen; the iPhone frame only on a real desktop (min 760 × 700 px with a mouse). The app is portrait only: sideways it shows "Turn your phone upright". Pinch zoom and larger text sizes work, and forms lift above the keypad (keyboard-safe sticky buttons, inputs at 16 px).
  • The free tunnel can stop and changes its address; the laptop alone always works.
  • iPhone notifications need the app on the Home Screen and notifications turned on inside the app.
  • Interest is credited monthly in the demo; in the proposal it accrues daily.
  • Simple Persely is not checked on a real iPhone yet (safe area, the chime with the silent switch, the haptic). The MP4 backups in media/ show the screens from before Simple Persely.
  • Weekly challenges exist in the code but are hidden from the tab to keep it short.
  • Undo is one chip above the tab bar for 10 seconds. It stays across tabs and pages and reverses only the move it belongs to (balances go back by the amount that moved, list items by id), so a later move is kept; a push already sent cannot be taken back.
  • Persi is our own model; for the real app OTP's official artwork should replace it.
  • English UI; the real app is Hungarian.
