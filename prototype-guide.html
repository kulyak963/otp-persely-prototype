Persely prototype guide

Everything that is in the Team 3 prototype for OTP Bank: what each screen does, the rules behind the numbers, how to run the demo, and where to change things in the code. Read this to review the prototype and to propose what to add before Friday.

OTP × Corvinus seminar · Team 3Demo data onlyEnglish UI

The idea

Problem from OTP: students leave when they turn 24 and the OTP Junior package ends.

Our answer: a new Persely tab inside the real OTP app. Persely already exists (340,000+ users, 0.01% interest). We give students a reason to save with OTP (3.0% for every Junior customer, automatically), a habit (weekly streak), a face (Persi, the green Persely piggy from OTP's own ads) and a reason to stay after 24 (the graduation bridge).

From the OTP research
  • 85% say saving early matters, only 27% use a savings product
  • Young people are cautious: show "withdraw any time, no fees, insured" everywhere
  • Income is irregular until about 25: weekly streak, any amount counts
  • Short, clear texts; no forced jokes
From Levente's handoff
  • 3.0% rate, 28% tax, first 500,000 Ft (we give it to every Junior customer, no sign-up)
  • Payday rule that asks first, round-ups, weekly transfers
  • Shared Persely, euro Persely, graduation bridge
  • Monthly insight on Home, semester recap, goal forecast
From the team
  • 1:1 copy of the OTP app, the new tab in their style
  • Persi in the tab and in notifications
  • Snapchat-style streak with a flame
  • Colourful monthly stories, "PS5 + GTA VI" goal

Open the prototype

Online, for everyone

otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com — the same app, hosted on Render, updated automatically from the GitHub repository kulyak963/otp-persely-prototype. On an iPhone: open it in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The free server sleeps after 15 idle minutes, so open the link a minute before presenting. Presenter tools online need the presenter key: …/?key=ADMIN_KEY once on the laptop, and …/push.html?key=ADMIN_KEY for the push panel (ask Denis for the key).

On the laptop

  1. Start the server: node server.js in the project folder (port 8090).
  2. Open http://localhost:8090. On a real desktop screen (wide, tall, with a mouse) the app sits in an iPhone frame, and the demo panel is on the left. On any phone, also in landscape, there is no frame; a phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi.
  3. Presenter panel for real pushes: http://localhost:8090/push.html (only works on this laptop).

On an iPhone

  1. Run the tunnel: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090. It prints an https address like …trycloudflare.com.
  2. Open that address in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the app from the icon → More → Notifications → Turn on notifications.

The free tunnel gets a new address every time it restarts. Then the app has to be added to the Home Screen again.

One Persely screen

Tap any goal.

  • Balance, progress, goal date
  • "At your pace you get there in April 2027" or "after your goal date", with the one-tap fix and, if the analyst is off, an offer to see how it fits your income
  • Add money / Take out. With 0 Ft inside, Edit goal (name, icon, amount, date, weekly transfer) and Delete goal (confirmation, then 5 second Undo; its rules move to the next goal or switch off) appear; with money inside, "Take all out" and a line saying to take it out first
  • Rate on this goal in Ft per year
  • Switches: payday rule, round-ups, weekly transfer go to this goal (same switch style, with Undo)
  • Shared: who put in what, rules, Invite a friend

  • Last 5 activity lines

Create a goal (3 steps)

Goals → New + → Your own goal (starts empty: you type the name; star icon), or a preset. Same amount and date controls as onboarding step 1 (typed amount, non-linear slider, month sheet); default date 10 months ahead.

  1. Name, amount, date
  2. Weekly transfer and round-ups
  3. Check: "With these rules: about 111,000 Ft by 1 September", short by X, Make it 3,000 Ft a week, Create

Sheets

  • Add money: goal chips, amount, from Junior or Savings account, streak hint
  • Withdraw: instant, no fee, streak not affected; shared = only your part
  • Payday: "+75,000 Ft arrived. Move 10% (7,500 Ft)?" with before → after
  • Payday rule / Weekly / Round-ups settings
  • Month picker: year stepper, quick years and a 3 × 4 month grid, up to 50 years ahead

One message at a time: banner, then sheet, then toast; no toast over a sheet or a button. Add money, Withdraw, payday Move, Accept split, rule changes and switches show Undo for 5 seconds; it puts back balances, streak, score and rules together.

Graduation screen

"Congratulations, Anna" (or "Your Junior package ends soon"). Three steps: give employer your OTP account number, first salary arrives at OTP within 6 months, Perselys keep 3.0% for 12 months. Tip to raise the payday rule. Keep my 3.0% turns the badge into Graduate 3.0%.

Analyst and health score

It answers the analyst promise in the opening script: Persi stops being only a tip card and helps the student plan the month.

FeatureWhat it does
Analyst, offered in contextOff by default (opt-in). It is offered where it helps: on a late goal's page, on Persi's monthly tip on Home, under My monthly spending on Home ("See how it fits your income"), on the Monthly spending page, and with a switch in More (the switch turns it off in one tap). Every entry opens one sheet: three short lines (it sorts your payments, finds your regular bills and suggests a monthly split; only from this OTP account, nothing moves without your tap; turn it off any time), then the suggested split, then two equal buttons, Turn on and Not now. A What it reads link keeps the full list (card payments, transfers, direct debits and income on this OTP account; the names of people you pay are only used to sort your payments). After Turn on the split shows with Use this split and Change it: three taps in all. The split is four buckets on one base, "Based on about 145,000 Ft a month in": by default Must-pay 42% 61,000 Ft, Everyday essentials 33% 47,500 Ft, Fun 20% 29,000 Ft, Saving 5% 7,500 Ft ("7,500 Ft = 10% of your salary"). With the payday rule and round-ups on (the main demo state, key J) it is 42 / 31 / 20 / 7 with Saving 10,500 Ft; with the weekly transfer too (the full demo path after Q) it is 42 / 26 / 20 / 12 with Saving 17,000 Ft. Undo for 5 seconds.
My splitA block on the Spending analysis page (under Safe to spend), shown only when the analyst is on. Four buckets on the one base, with live use, e.g. "Fun · 20%, 7,890 Ft of 29,000 Ft, 27% used · 11 days left". The edit sheet starts from the suggestion. Everyday essentials is what is left of the income after the other three. The Saving bucket is your saving rules (payday rule plus weekly transfer and round-ups), so the two always show the same number; "7,500 Ft = 10% of your salary" says how the payday rule relates to the base. The reward rule uses the same buckets. The edit sheet also has Of Saving, for investing (a stepper in 1,000 Ft, starting at 0 Ft, never more than Saving): a plan only, nothing is bought and no money moves; it shows as an Investing line under Saving on the monthly plan.
RecategoriseWith the analyst on, card payments in Transactions are tappable. The student can move one to another category, and the split and Persi's tips follow.
Financial health score0–100, shown as the bottom row of the Your habits card ("N of 100 · Financial health score · ▲ +N since last month"); tap it for the four indicators and the Rewards list. Four indicators, 25 points each, over a rolling 3 months: spends less than earns; no overdraft or failed payment; a buffer of about one month of spending; saves regularly toward a named goal. A line under the score explains it ("Spending under income · no overdraft · a buffer · regular saving"), and the story shows it once, as "75 of 100 · 3 of 4 habits held · ▲ 25".
RewardsEarned on improvement or on staying within the split, never on balance size. For students: boosted partner cashback (5% next month, up to 2,000 Ft) and goal-linked partner offers (reaching Summer holiday: travel partner, 8% back on one booking, up to 10,000 Ft; any other goal: 5% boosted cashback, up to 2,000 Ft) (a fee waiver would only make sense for OTP GO customers, 24–28, because Junior accounts are already free).
Score-change storyPart of the "Your progress" slide in the monthly story (analyst on and split accepted): the health score counts up from last month's to this month's, shown once, next to the goal ring and the streak.

Proposal only: the split percentages, the score design, the rewards and all rates are team proposals, not OTP facts. Demo order: turn on the analyst after the first deposit, then press O a month later. With the split accepted (key J state), Anna lands Everyday essentials 36,900 of 44,500 Ft and Fun 27,000 of 29,000 Ft. Messages come one at a time: Persi's push "Reward earned…" (it also goes to the iPhone if Also send to iPhone is on), then the "Reward earned" sheet (score 50 → 75, 5% partner cashback next month), then the summary toast. The monthly story shows the score on its progress slide.

It moves exactly the unpaid 47,550 Ft to the Bills pocket plus the 7,500 Ft saving rule, and says what follows: "Then: bills covered · 7,500 Ft to Summer holiday · 19,950 Ft free for Fun" (the goal name is the rule's goal). Below it Only cover the bills and a text link Not this time. One Undo restores both moves. The push reads "Salary's in: +75,000 Ft. 47,550 Ft of bills still to pay."
Bills pocketA pot for bills only on the Must-pay bills page: no goal, no interest, held outside the Junior balance. Moving money in is an own-account move (it is not spending and not income). The button on the Bills pocket block, "Move 61,000 Ft to Bills pocket", is a month of must-pay bills; it can be moved back at any time. The payday sheet moves only what is unpaid.

Nothing is counted twice: every transaction is either income, spending in one category, an own-account move (Persely, Savings, Bills pocket, interest), cash or a top-up to another app. Own-account moves are never income and never spending. The self-check window.__checkAnalytics() in the browser console runs the checks: all must pass (134 of 134 at the time of writing).

Demo data and proposals: the 3-month history behind these screens (August to October, about 60 payments a month, and the bills) is made-up demo data, the same on every Reset. The projection weights, the 7-day and 15% limits, the groceries rule and the Bills pocket are team proposals, not OTP facts and not bank data.

Monthly story

It starts by itself when opened (no start tap), with a 2-second title ("Your month · December · In about 30 seconds"). Eight screens in the demo story (7 slides and the share card), one idea each, on one deep-green palette; every figure is computed from the transactions and the analytics, nothing is typed in.

  • In and out: "In" in neutral white, "Out" in the bucket colours; hero "9,600 Ft left after the bills still due (5,550 Ft)", with a dashed "Bills due" segment. An overspent month shows a hatched block above a dashed In line and "over by …".
  • Where it went: the donut is a share of the money that came in (centre "came in 145,000 Ft"): Must-pay 38%, Everyday essentials 25%, Fun 19%, Saving 7%, Left over 11%. With the split accepted every row also shows "plan N%". The hero is the part Anna can steer: "Within your plan · Fun 19%, plan 20%", never Must-pay.
  • Bills: a receipt. A late bill moves this slide to second place: "Rent share is late" ("N bills are late"), "42,000 Ft, due on the 2nd. Phone 5,550 Ft is due in 6 days.", with Pay now. Row tags: Late, Due now, Today, In 6 days. When nothing is late: "5 of 6 bills paid · Phone is due later: 5,550 Ft". Semester: "Payments made 22 of 22".
  • Biggest change: only when a category is 25% or more above its 3-month average (line chart; a skipped month is a gap; "3-month average 12,153 Ft"). In the semester and year stories this is best and hardest month, with a forint value on the bars and ▲ / ▼ on the best and the hardest.
  • What you put aside: "Rules and round-ups moved 10,500 Ft. You added … Interest added 271 Ft." ("You added" only when she added money). Interest only: "Interest only. Nothing moved in this month."
  • Your progress: the goal ring and one line ("On pace for April 2027", counted on the demo clock; the year story says November 2028), the streak, and the health score once ("75 of 100 · 3 of 4 habits held · ▲ 25"). A goal whose date has gone: "PS5 + GTA VI needs a new date. Your 46,083 Ft is safe." with a Pick a new date button that opens that goal. The reward line shows only when no goal is late.
  • Next month: "Based on about 145,000 Ft a month in", the four buckets, and one tip from tipOf() with four outcomes. A late bill: "Pay rent share first / 42,000 Ft, due on the 2nd. Your saving tips wait until it is paid." with Pay now. An overspent month: "A tighter month", no button. Normal: "Move 1,100 Ft · 10% of food and drink, about 11,467 Ft a month, to Summer holiday." with Set it up, then "Done: 1,100 Ft a month". No tip: the plan only. With the analyst off the slide is titled "One tip" and keeps only the tip.
  • Share card without amounts: leads with the best goal, no 0-week streak, no goal whose date has passed; when there is nothing to share it says "Nothing to share this month yet." Toast: "Share card ready. It shows no amounts".

Spoken text (VoiceOver) is the text on screen, at most about 25 words a slide. A slide lasts 2.5 s plus 0.17 s per word on screen, capped at 7 s. Measured with __story.info(): demo story 29.8 s, rent unpaid 31.3 s, semester 36.7 s, year story 23.9 s (the title says "about 30", "about 35" and "about 25" seconds). Tap right or left to move, press and hold to pause, swipe down to close; the pause button and the arrow keys also pause (the button then shows Play). Bills and next month need the analyst (it is opt-in): with it off they are left out. The friends rank is not in the story. After "End of semester" it becomes the semester story: the same slides over the semester.

Persi and notifications

There are ten notification keys: 1 to 9 and P. Persi is our own 3D version of the green Persely piggy (three.js). It appears in the tab header, the update pop-up, the onboarding summary (a coin drops in), the hint strips on the Persely tab, the graduation screen, the story and the Home card. It speaks in one short, friendly line, without slang. Persely notifications are signed "Persi · Persely". The +1 month push "Reward earned: …" comes before the reward sheet and reaches the iPhone if Also send to iPhone is on. On the laptop the banner shows Persi's portrait; a real iPhone always shows the app icon.

How it is built

No framework, no database. The whole app is one HTML file that runs in the browser; a tiny Node server serves it and sends push notifications. State lives in the browser (localStorage key otp-clone-en-v5), so every phone has its own demo.

FileWhat it is
index.htmlThe whole app: styles, screens, rules, onboarding, story, 3D piggy, demo panel.
server.jsNode server on port 8090. Serves only listed files. Push API: /api/vapid, /api/subscribe, /api/test; laptop-only /api/push, /api/subs, /api/clear.

Limits to know

  • Demo only: no real accounts, money or bank backend.
  • Phone layout for every touch screen; the iPhone frame only on a real desktop (min 760 × 700 px with a mouse). The app is portrait only: sideways it shows "Turn your phone upright". Pinch zoom and larger text sizes work, and forms lift above the keypad (keyboard-safe sticky buttons, inputs at 16 px).
  • The free tunnel can stop and changes its address; the laptop alone always works.
  • iPhone notifications need the app on the Home Screen and notifications turned on inside the app.
  • Interest is credited monthly in the demo; in the proposal it accrues daily.
  • Weekly challenges exist in the code but are hidden from the tab to keep it short.
  • Undo restores the whole state saved before the action, so anything else changed in those 5 seconds is undone too; a push already sent cannot be taken back.
  • Persi is our own model; for the real app OTP's official artwork should replace it.
  • English UI; the real app is Hungarian.
