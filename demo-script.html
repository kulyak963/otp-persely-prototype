Persely demo script

Team 3, OTP × Corvinus seminar. One year with Kiss Anna in the Simple Persely app: from her first login to the end of her first year. About 5 and a half minutes of demo inside a 10 minute pitch (demo 2:00 to 7:30, close at 7:30). Time jumps play on the app's own screens: balances roll, jars fill and a small bar shows the month and each event. Every figure below was read off the app on 20 November 2026 in a 390 × 844 run from a clean start (U), with the taps shown.

Before you start

  1. Two minutes before: open otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com so the free server wakes up. On the laptop open it once as …/?key=ADMIN_KEY so real pushes work. Then open the presenter address …/?demo=1&view=simple (with the key if needed). ?demo=1 shows the demo panel left of the phone and turns the keys on (remembered; ?demo=0 removes it; H hides the panel). ?view=simple makes sure the new Persely tab shows even after X (an existing user otherwise opens on the Classic tab with a "Try the new Persely" card). The panel button Persely view switches Classic and simple at any time. Backup: localhost:8090 with the same address.
  2. Press U (or Reset app): the new user start: fresh data, the lock screen with a Face ID unlock, the splash (a tap skips it), then Home. Anna has 87 450 Ft on her Junior account, 0 Ft in Persely, no goal, no streak; her Junior package ends in 300 days (month 10 of the year). X switches to the existing user (Summer holiday, PS5 + GTA VI, Festival ticket, 140 000 Ft, week 9) and back.
  3. One Persely push a day. All Persely pushes count together per app day; a second one goes only to the inbox and a toast says "Held by the 1 a day limit". The "Reward earned" push after O uses up that day, so press Z (Reset day) right before P, or the salary push will not show as a banner. The late bill push (B) is not part of the cap.
  4. Only one message shows at a time (banner, then sheet, then toast). After a push banner its sheet follows when the banner slides away; tap the banner to open it at once. Every money move shows Undo for 10 seconds, also when you change tab; do not tap it by accident.
  5. The fast forward plays on its own; a tap on the dark bar skips to its summary sheet. The driver never talks during an animation.
  6. Presenting from the iPhone only: open the app with ?demo=1&view=simple (or tap "Persely prototype" at the bottom of Settings five times), then long press Anna's picture on Home for the same controls; a plain tap on it opens Settings.
  7. Pitch notes. Face ID is demo only. The first run tiles are Trip, Phone or tech, Safety net and Something else; Own home is under More goals (decision D10), so the house plan takes one tap more. There is no Quick actions card, no bell, no analyst card and no Safe to spend card on Home any more: Home is the original OTP layout with one Persely card, and the analyst, Safe to spend and the late bill card live on the Spending page (Home > Details). The Persely tab has no hint strips and no grey story ring. Avoid shell rows in More and Transfer: they show the "not part of this demo" toast.

If something breaks

  • The online link is slow or down: wait 50 seconds (it was asleep), or run the server on the laptop and present from localhost:8090.
  • Wrong state on screen: U or Reset app restarts from act 1 (Face ID). X switches between the new user and the existing user.
  • A push did not show as a banner: it was held by the 1 a day limit. Press Z and repeat the key; the held one is in Settings > Notifications.
  • The app fails: play media/ff-1-month.mp4, ff-2-months.mp4, ff-year-1-to-junior-ends.mp4, ff-year-2-to-month-12.mp4 and story-november.mp4 in that order and narrate over them. They are older recordings: they show the Home and Persely tab from before Simple Persely (Safe to spend card on Home, goal cards on the tab) and the story before Denis's version. The fast forward summary figures match this script (2 000 Ft path); say "an earlier build" if asked.
  • A phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi; turn it back. The iPhone frame appears only on a real desktop screen (wide, tall and with a mouse), never on a phone.
  • A message seems missing: only one shows at a time; the next one comes when the first is closed.
  • The new tab misbehaves on stage: the panel button Persely view switches to the Classic tab, or reload with ?simple=0 for the whole old app (the older numbers and acts then apply).
