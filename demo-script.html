Persely demo script

Team 3, OTP × Corvinus seminar. One student, Kiss Anna, from the update pop-up to the end of her Junior package. About 6 minutes. Everything runs in our app on the iPhone and on the laptop next to it.

Before you start

  1. Laptop: run the server and open localhost:8090 on the projector. The demo panel sits to the left of the phone; H hides it.
  2. iPhone: open the app from the Home Screen icon. Turn on Also send to iPhone in the panel if pushes should reach the phone too.
  3. Press U to start from the update day. If something goes wrong, Reset app in the panel jumps to the state "8 weeks later".
  4. Presenting from the iPhone only: long-press Anna's picture on Home to open the same demo controls.

Run sheet

1

The update 0:00

Press U. The pop-up with Persi asks "What are you saving for?". Tap the first tile, PS5 + GTA 6 (marked Trending). Give the room a second to react.

"Persely already has more than 340,000 users, but it pays 0.01%. Students want to save: 85% say it matters, only 27% use a savings product."

2

Student rate 0:40

Onboarding step 1. Point at the two lines: today about 5 Ft a year, with the student rate about 1,380 Ft. Tap Verify with Neptun.

"3.0% on the first 500,000 Ft for verified students, about 2.2% after tax. Revolut pays 1.8% after tax."

3

Goal and forecast 1:10

PS5 + GTA 6, 230,000 Ft, Dec 2027 → Next. Payday rule 10% and round-ups are on. The card says about 131,000 Ft by 1 December 2027, 99,000 short. Tap Add 2,000 Ft a week to make it.

"Real goals, in students' own words. Every goal has a date, and the app tells you honestly whether you will make it. Late goals are marked in orange."

4

First deposit 1:50

Pick 1,000 Ft → Continue → choose the account in Pay from → Confirm. A coin drops into Persi. Skip the short tour or tap through it.

"The streak starts with any amount. It is weekly, because student income is irregular."

5

Payday P 2:20

Persi's push: "Salary's in: +75,000 Ft. Move 7,500 Ft to PS5 + GTA 6?" The sheet shows the goal before and after. Tap Move 7,500 Ft.

"The app asks first. The student keeps control, and the share is gone before it can be spent."

6

Round-up C 2:50

A 1,190 Ft coffee. The toast shows a 20 Ft round-up going to the goal.

"Small change, doubled. Persely already rounds up today; we connect it to the goal."

7

Shared Persely D 3:10

Persely tab → Shared Persely button under the goals → Create and invite. Press D: Dóri joins with 15,000 Ft and the Persely screen shows who put in what.

"Flatmates save for Balaton together. Everyone can always take back what they put in; paying out the whole pot needs everyone."

8

A month later O 3:40

Salary, rules, card payments and interest run. Open Home: Persi's tip says food was the biggest category and 10% of it brings the goal weeks closer. Tap Move 10% a month.

"Insight with one action, not a report to read."

9

Streak reminder L then 3 4:10

Lock the phone, send push 3. It lands on the lock screen: "Your 8-week streak ends Sunday." Tap it to open the app.

"One reminder a week at most, from Persi, not from 'OTP Bank'."

10

End of semester E 4:30

The semester story opens: colourful slides, interest earned, and a share card that shows the streak and goal progress without amounts.

"Something students want to post, and it says Saving with OTP Persely."

11

Junior ends G 5:10

"Congratulations, Anna." Three steps. Tap Keep my 3.0%; the badge turns into Graduate 3.0%.

"This is the moment the bank loses students today. The rate continues for 12 months if the first salary comes to OTP."

Closing line: students choose it because it pays, it builds on Persely, and it keeps them with OTP after the Junior package ends.

If something breaks

  • The iPhone link stops working: the free tunnel address changes when it restarts. Run cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090 again and reinstall the app from the new link, or present from the laptop only.
  • Wrong state on screen: Reset app in the panel, or U to restart from the update day.
  • No projector cable: the laptop panel and the phone frame work on any screen share.
