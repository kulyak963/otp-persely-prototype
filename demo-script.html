Persely demo script

Team 3, OTP × Corvinus seminar. One year with Kiss Anna: from her first login to the end of her first year with Persely. About 7 minutes of demo inside a 10-minute pitch. Time jumps play on the app's own screens: balances roll, bars grow and a small bar shows the month and each event, so the jury sees Anna's real money move. Everything runs in our app on the laptop (iPhone frame) and on the iPhone.

Before you start

  1. Two minutes before: open otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com so the free server wakes up. On the laptop open it once as …/?key=ADMIN_KEY so real pushes work. The demo panel sits to the left of the phone; H hides it. (Backup: localhost:8090, then the MP4s in media/.)
  2. Press U (or Reset app): the new-user start: fresh data, the lock screen with a Face ID unlock, the splash (a tap skips it), then Home. Anna has 0 Ft in Persely, no goal and no streak, and her Junior package ends in 300 days (month 10 of the year). To run the pitch as the existing user instead (Summer holiday, PS5, Festival ticket, an 8-week streak), press X or the Seed button in the panel; press it again to go back to the new user.
  3. Only one message shows at a time (banner, then sheet, then toast); once Anna has a goal, the Persi hint strips on the Persely tab show on each block's first appearance and count as seen after a second on screen. After a push banner the sheet follows in about 4 seconds; tap the banner to open it at once. Every money move shows Undo for 5 seconds; do not tap it by accident. The Undo toast closes by itself when you open another page.
  4. The fast-forward plays on its own; a tap on the dark bar skips to its summary sheet. The driver never talks during an animation.
  5. Presenting from the iPhone only: long-press Anna's picture on Home for the same controls (+1 month, +2 months, To month 12, Bill missed, Demo start).
  6. Pitch notes: Face ID is demo only. Start the first goal from the tiles on the Persely tab (Home has the card "Start your first goal"), not from New +. On stage avoid shell rows. Every money sheet (Add money, Withdraw, New transfer) now opens empty: type the amount or tap a chip (and for a transfer a name and a 16 or 24 digit account number), and anything above 20,000 Ft asks for a review first. The Undo chip stays for 10 seconds across tabs. Show the Spending analysis page from the Safe to spend card on Home.

"She picks a real goal: her first home. The app tells her straight away what it takes each week."

3

Auto savings 2:45 · 30 s

Step 2: payday rule 10% and round-ups are on. The card says about 319,000 Ft by 1 June 2029, 181,000 short. If a row says "Reminders are off. Turn on notifications to get your Sunday reminder.", leave it (the browser would ask for permission). Create goal. A coin drops into Persi.

"Three switches, and saving runs on its own. She never has to remember."

Financial analytics 4:10 · 50 s

Home → See how it fits your income. One sheet, three lines (sorts your payments and finds your bills; only from this OTP account, nothing moves without your tap; turn it off any time), then the suggested split on one base, "Based on about 145,000 Ft a month in": Must-pay 42% 61,000 Ft, Everyday essentials 26% 38,000 Ft, Fun 20% 29,000 Ft, Saving 12% 17,000 Ft ("7,500 Ft = 10% of your salary + 6,500 Ft weekly transfer + 3,000 Ft round-ups"). Two equal buttons: Turn on and Not now; then Use this split (or Change it). Three taps in all. Home now has one Safe to spend card ("Coming up: Phone 5,550 Ft on the 20th", 176,550 Ft): tap it for the Spending analysis page: the month's circle (14,480 Ft spent so far), the categories, the monthly plan with Persi's tips, then Safe to spend, My split, Next month, Must-pay bills and Bills pocket (on the Persely tab it is the one Spending analysis row). Tap Next month: the same four buckets on the same base, with no "Spending is above income" line (the plan fits: about 3,600 Ft is left over). Back, tap Confirm my bills: "We found 8 regular payments", the switches under a "Must-pay" label, rent, utilities, the pass and the phone already ticked, "Paid 2 Feb" chips; this month 55,450 Ft paid, the phone due in 6 days.

"The analyst sorts every payment, locks the bills she must pay and projects next month from her last three."

Tap See your year story: it starts by itself with a 2-second title ("Your year · Dec 2026 – Nov 2027 · 12 months, in about 25 seconds"), then 7 slides in about 24 seconds: in and out, where it went (share of the money that came in), best and hardest month, what she put aside, progress, and the share card without amounts ("Nothing moved" months say "Interest only. If something breaks

  • The online link is slow or down: wait 50 seconds (it was asleep), or run the server on the laptop and present from localhost:8090.
  • Wrong state on screen: U or Reset app restarts from act 1 (Face ID). J still gives the "8 weeks later" state, and X switches between the new user and the existing user.
  • The app fails: play media/ff-1-month.mp4, ff-2-months.mp4, ff-year-1-to-junior-ends.mp4, ff-year-2-to-month-12.mp4 and story-november.mp4 in that order and narrate over them.
  • A phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi; turn it back. The iPhone frame appears only on a real desktop screen (wide, tall and with a mouse), never on a phone.
  • A message seems missing: only one shows at a time; the next one comes when the first is closed.
  • No projector cable: the laptop panel and the phone frame work on any screen share.
