Persely demo script

Team 3, OTP × Corvinus seminar. One year with Kiss Anna: from her first login to the end of her first year with Persely. About 7 minutes of demo inside a 10-minute pitch. Time jumps play on the app's own screens: balances roll, bars grow and a small bar shows the month and each event, so the jury sees Anna's real money move. Everything runs in our app on the laptop (iPhone frame) and on the iPhone.

Before you start

  1. Two minutes before: open otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com so the free server wakes up. On the laptop open it once as …/?key=ADMIN_KEY so real pushes work. The demo panel sits to the left of the phone when the address ends in ?demo=1 (for example …/?demo=1&key=ADMIN_KEY; the flag is remembered, ?demo=0 removes it); H hides it. Without the flag the panel and the keys are off, as for a real user. (Backup: localhost:8090, then the MP4s in media/.)
  2. Press U (or Reset app): the new-user start: fresh data, the lock screen with a Face ID unlock, the splash (a tap skips it), then Home. Anna has 87 450 Ft on her Junior account, 0 Ft in Persely, no goal and no streak, and her Junior package ends in 300 days (month 10 of the year). To run the pitch as the existing user instead (Summer holiday, PS5, Festival ticket, an 8-week streak), press X or the Seed button in the panel; press it again to go back to the new user.
  3. Only one message shows at a time (banner, then sheet, then toast); once Anna has a goal, the Persi hint strips on the Persely tab show on each block's first appearance and count as seen after a second on screen. After a push banner the sheet follows in about 4 seconds; tap the banner to open it at once. Every money move shows Undo for 10 seconds, also when you change tab; do not tap it by accident.
  4. The fast-forward plays on its own; a tap on the dark bar skips to its summary sheet. The driver never talks during an animation.
  5. Presenting from the iPhone only: open the app with ?demo=1 (or tap "Persely prototype" at the bottom of Settings five times), then long-press Anna's picture on Home for the same controls; a plain tap on it opens Settings (+1 month, +2 months, To month 12, Bill missed, Demo start).
  6. Pitch notes: Face ID is demo only. Start the first goal from the tiles on the Persely tab, or from the Home card "Start your first goal", which opens the same tiles in a sheet. Avoid the shell rows in More and Transfer: they show the "not part of this demo" toast. New + opens the same tiles and the same wizard, so either works. On stage avoid shell rows. Every money sheet (Add money, Withdraw, New transfer) now opens empty: type the amount or tap a chip (and for a transfer a name and a 16 or 24 digit account number), and anything above 20 000 Ft asks for a review first. The Undo chip stays for 10 seconds across tabs. Show the one Spending page from the Safe to spend card on Home.

"Three switches, and saving runs on its own. She never has to remember. And before she creates it, one page says rate, cap, tax and fees in plain words."

If something breaks

  • The online link is slow or down: wait 50 seconds (it was asleep), or run the server on the laptop and present from localhost:8090.
  • Wrong state on screen: U or Reset app restarts from act 1 (Face ID). J still gives the "8 weeks later" state, and X switches between the new user and the existing user.
  • The app fails: play media/ff-1-month.mp4, ff-2-months.mp4, ff-year-1-to-junior-ends.mp4, ff-year-2-to-month-12.mp4 and story-november.mp4 in that order and narrate over them.
  • A phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi; turn it back. The iPhone frame appears only on a real desktop screen (wide, tall and with a mouse), never on a phone.
  • A message seems missing: only one shows at a time; the next one comes when the first is closed.
  • No projector cable: the laptop panel and the phone frame work on any screen share.
