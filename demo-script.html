Persely demo script

Team 3, OTP × Corvinus seminar. One year with Kiss Anna: from her first login to the end of her first year with Persely. About 7 minutes of demo inside a 10-minute pitch. Time jumps play on the app's own screens: balances roll, bars grow and a small bar shows the month and each event, so the jury sees Anna's real money move. Everything runs in our app on the laptop (iPhone frame) and on the iPhone.

Before you start

  1. Two minutes before: open otp-persely-prototype.onrender.com so the free server wakes up. On the laptop open it once as …/?key=ADMIN_KEY so real pushes work. The demo panel sits to the left of the phone; H hides it. On the right sits Behind the app: each thing Anna does opens a card with what the bank does behind it, in plain words (salary → payday rule → push → the bank moves the money → goal and streak update). A hides it. Open the app in a full-width browser window (980 px or more), otherwise it shows as a plain phone screen without the panels.
  2. Press U (or Reset app): fresh data, the lock screen with a Face ID unlock, the splash, then Persi's pop-up. Anna has no First home fund yet and her Junior package ends in 300 days (month 10 of the year).
  3. Only one message shows at a time (banner, then sheet, then toast); right after the first goal Persi's tour starts: the screen dims and lights up one block at a time (savings, goals, streak, money in, monthly story), Persi explains each in a card. Tap Next through it (about 15 seconds) or Skip. After a push banner the sheet follows in about 4 seconds; tap the banner to open it at once. Every money move shows Undo for 5 seconds; do not tap it by accident. The Undo toast closes by itself when you open another page.
  4. The fast-forward plays on its own; a tap on the dark bar skips to its summary sheet. The driver never talks during an animation.
  5. Presenting from the iPhone only: long-press Anna's picture on Home for the same controls (+1 month, +2 months, To month 12, Bill missed, Demo start).

0

Opening 0:00 · 2 min

Speaker 1, no app. The opening script (with the fixes from the plan: 0.01% "almost nothing", and OTP's own problem at 24).

"…Let me hand over to the others, who'll show you how it actually works."

First login U 2:00 · 15 s

Lock screen, Face ID scans and unlocks, the OTP splash, Home. Persi's pop-up: "What are you saving for?"

"Anna opens the OTP app like every day. Persely is just there."

2

House plan 2:15 · 30 s

Tap Own home (Trending). Step 1: First home fund, 500,000 Ft by June 2029; the line above Next says "≈ 3,800 Ft a week". Optional, a few seconds: drag the amount slider or open "By when?" for the month sheet. Tap Next.

"She picks a real goal: her first home. The app tells her straight away what it takes each week."

3

Auto savings 2:45 · 30 s

Step 2: payday rule 10% and round-ups are on. The card says about 319,000 Ft by 1 June 2029, 181,000 short. Tap Add 1,500 Ft a week to make it: the weekly transfer switches on and the card turns to "You reach 500,000 Ft in May 2029, before June 2029 · On time". Next → Check and create, 1,000 Ft first deposit preselected. If a row says "Reminders are off. Turn on notifications to get your Sunday reminder.", leave it (the browser would ask for permission). Create goal. A coin drops into Persi.

"Three switches, and saving runs on its own. She never has to remember."

4

Fast forward: 1 month O 3:15 · 15 s

The month runs on Anna's own Home screen: the Junior account rises with the salary and the family transfer (67,000 Ft from Kiss István on the 1st) and falls with the bills and card payments, Persely · total climbs, while the dark bar above the tab bar shows December 2026, the day running and each event (+75,000 Ft Salary, +70,000 Ft Family transfer, −55,450 Ft 5 bills paid, +16,500 Ft To Persely, +266 Ft Interest). It ends on a summary sheet: "December 2026 · 1 month later": in 145,000 Ft, bills 5 of 5 on time, 16,500 Ft put aside, +266 Ft interest, 147,766 Ft in Persely. Tap Continue.

"Let's skip a month. Watch where her money goes."

5

New salary P 3:30 · 25 s

Persi's push: "Salary's in: +75,000 Ft. Move 7,500 Ft to First home fund?" Tap the banner; the sheet shows the goal before and after. Tap Move 7,500 Ft.

"Payday. The app asks first, and the share is gone before it can be spent."

6

Fast forward: 2 months Q 3:55 · 15 s

January runs on Home, February on the Persely tab: the total and the goal bars grow. The summary sheet says "Jan 2027 – Feb 2027 · 2 months later": money in 290,000 Ft, bills 10 of 10 on time, 33,000 Ft put aside, +648 Ft interest, 188,913 Ft in Persely. Continue.

"Two more months. Now the bank has three months of her real money to learn from."

7

Left to spend 4:10 · 30 s

Home: the spending circle and the categories are the real OTP app. Point at the one line we add under the circle: Left to spend this month. Tap it. One screen: the big number with "about N Ft a day", then How we count it as a short ladder: on your Junior account, minus bills still to pay, minus saving still to move, minus groceries for the rest of the month, equals left to spend. Under it the bills still to pay and Persi's tip with one button. At the bottom, for students who want more: Full analysis (do not open it on stage unless asked).

"OTP already shows what you spent. We add the one number students actually need: how much is left to spend after the bills and saving. And we show how we got it."

8

Bill missed B 4:40 · 25 s

Persi's push: "Rent share is late: 42,000 Ft (due on the 2nd). Left to spend this month: …". Tap it: Left to spend opens with the late rent on top of the bills and Pay now → Pay 42,000 Ft. Toast: "Rent share paid".

"If a bill is not paid, Persi says so before the money goes on fun. It only tells: nothing is blocked."

9

Fast forward: to month 12 Y 5:30 · 15 s

The year runs on the Persely tab, each month faster: the total, the yearly interest line and the goal bars grow, the bar shows what went to Persely and the interest. It stops at month 10: "Dec 2026 – Sep 2027 · Your Junior package ends".

"Now a whole year."

10

Junior ends 5:45 · 20 s

Tap See what's next → "Congratulations, Anna", three steps → Keep my 3.0%. The badge turns into Graduate 3.0% and the year carries on by itself to month 12: "Dec 2026 – Nov 2027 · One year with Persely", 60 of 60 bills on time, 205,500 Ft put aside, +5,340 Ft interest, 341,837 Ft in Persely.

"This is the moment banks lose students. Anna keeps 3.0% because her salary stays at OTP."

11

A year of earnings 5:50 · 30 s

Tap See your year. Three things only: the money in Persely now and what she put aside this year; What your rate earned, two bars: Persely at 3.0% +5,340 Ft against the same money at 0.01% +18 Ft; 341,837 Ft in Persely, 205,500 Ft put aside; and her goals with their progress.

"One year: this is what saving with OTP earned her, and what the same money earned at 0.01%."

12

Monthly story 6:20 · 35 s

Back on the Persely tab, tap the green story circle next to Persi. Spotify Wrapped style, one fact per slide, each its own colour: her top places (like top artists), her spending day, days without spending, her biggest buy, her subscriptions a month and a year, Wolt orders, then what went into Persely (Persi catches a coin), her goal and her streak, and the share card without amounts. Hold to pause, tap left or right to go back or on, swipe down to close.

"And once a month, something she actually wants to share."

13

Business case and close 7:25 · 1 min 30 s

Speaker 3, one slide: 10,000 users × 100,000 Ft = 1 billion Ft deposits; about 30 million Ft interest, about 20 million Ft funding value, net about 10 million Ft; about 500 extra retained customers break even.

"Students choose it because it pays, it builds on Persely, and it keeps them with OTP after the Junior package ends."

Extras if the slot is longer (about 20 s each): Shared Persely with Dóri (the Balaton trip is already in the goals with Anna's 20,000 Ft; press D: Dóri joins with 15,000 Ft and the goal page shows who put in what), coffee round-up (C), streak push on the lock screen (L then 3). Do them before Y. Monthly story: key M (same slides as the year story, for one month). If time is short, skip the optional slider in act 2; never skip acts 8, 10 and 11.

If something breaks

  • The online link is slow or down: wait 50 seconds (it was asleep), or run the server on the laptop and present from localhost:8090.
  • Wrong state on screen: U or Reset app restarts from act 1 (Face ID). J still gives the old "8 weeks later" state.
  • The laptop fails: present from the iPhone. Long-press Anna's picture on Home for the same demo controls (+1 month, +2 months, To month 12, Bill missed, Demo start).
  • A phone held sideways shows a "Turn your phone upright" card with Persi; turn it back. The iPhone frame appears only on a real desktop screen (wide, tall and with a mouse), never on a phone.
  • A message seems missing: only one shows at a time; the next one comes when the first is closed.
  • No projector cable: the laptop panel and the phone frame work on any screen share.
